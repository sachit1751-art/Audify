## Product truth

Audify is an Android YouTube Music client for playback, discovery, library organization, live synced lyrics, AI-powered lyrics translation, music recognition, and real-time listening together. The campaign should present it as a music app with an AI-powered lyric-translation feature—not as a generic AI assistant.

**Core idea:** One ink-blue/violet music pulse travels through discovery, recognition, playback, lyrics, and listening together, then resolves into Audify’s own app mark.

**Sayable device:** “One beat finds a song, follows its words, and brings people together.”

**Story format:** Fixed-hero journey / feature mission. The pulse remains the visual anchor as the world and real product screens change around it. Use three larger printed-window transitions at most; other beat changes come from camera movement, layered plates, and motion within the shot.

## Visual direction

- **Medium:** Three-plate risograph on warm cream stock; visible halftone dots, restrained plate misregistration, occasional ink dropout, rough circular windows, sharp printed typography.
- **Palette:** Use the shipped Riso palette—teal, fluorescent pink, yellow, and their overprints—with the blue-violet overprint reserved for the recurring music pulse. The hero color always means the beat/music.
- **Cinematic feel:** Deep negative space, deliberate macro-to-wide scale changes, slow push-ins, precise beat-synced movement, and one silence before the reveal. Premium through composition and restraint, not gradients, glow effects, or generic particle bursts.
- **Real UI only:** Use the actual Audify screenshots as printed objects/cards; never invent fake app screens. Candidate assets: `fastlane/metadata/android/en-US/images/screenshots/screenshot_1.png` (Home/quick picks), `screenshot_3.png` (Recognize Music), `screenshot_4.png` (Listen Together), `screenshot_5.png` (player, tightly cropped to controls), and `screenshot_7.png` (library/auto-playlists). Use `fastlane/metadata/android/en-US/images/icon.png` for the real app mark.
- **Rights and clarity:** Do not show legible third-party song lyrics or make another artist’s album art the focus. Use abstract glyphs for lyric motion and crop the player UI to controls. No YouTube/Google logos or implied endorsement. Keep the closing note clear that Audify is independent.
- **Phone safe area:** Keep essential content within approximately x=60–940, y=250–1500 on the 1080×1920 canvas; subtitles/captions at least 50 px.
