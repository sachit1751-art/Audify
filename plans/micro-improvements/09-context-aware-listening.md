# 09 — Context-Aware Listening

**Status:** Proposal — extend Plans 07 and 079; do not create a second recommendation-ranking engine.

## Problem
Recommendations can feel disconnected from the user's current listening intent, such as wanting familiar music, something new, or a particular listening mood.

## Brief solution
Add lightweight, user-controlled listening intents that adjust existing recommendation or radio inputs. Make the controls explicit, explain why suggestions appear, and keep sensitive inference off by default.

## Expected outcome
Discovery feels more relevant to the session while users retain control over what signals influence suggestions.

**Acceptance signal:** users can change or disable intent controls, and evaluations show improved relevance without reducing diversity or overriding explicit choices.
