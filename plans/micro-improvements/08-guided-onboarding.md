# 08 — Guided Onboarding

**Status:** Proposal — audit current first-run experience before implementation.

## Problem
New users may not understand what Audify supports, where to begin, or why permissions and provider connections are requested.

## Brief solution
Create a short, skippable first-run path that explains core capabilities, asks only for permissions at the point they are needed, and helps users reach a first successful play. Avoid mandatory account creation for features that do not require an account.

## Expected outcome
New users understand the app faster and can start listening without unnecessary setup or permission prompts.

**Acceptance signal:** first-play completion, onboarding abandonment, and permission-denial recovery are measured; returning users are not repeatedly interrupted.
