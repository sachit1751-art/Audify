Audify is an open-source music player for Android, Android TV and Wear OS.

Discover and organize music, manage playlists, play local audio, follow synchronized lyrics and customize playback.

FEATURES

• Music discovery: Search for songs, albums, artists and playlists.
• Personal library: Access supported personalized content.
• Playlist management: Create and organize playlists and manage the listening queue.
• Local music: Browse and play audio stored on your device.
• Background playback: Keep listening while using other apps.
• Audio controls: Gapless playback, crossfade, playback speed, silence skipping and sleep timer.
• Synchronized lyrics: Follow timed lyrics and navigate playback through them.
• Offline listening: Save supported audio for local playback.
• Customization: Material 3 interface, artwork-based colors and multiple launcher icons.
• Multi-device support: Dedicated Android TV and Wear OS experiences.

Audify is an independent third-party client and is not affiliated with or endorsed by Google or YouTube. Availability of connected services can vary.
